# Design System: Bao Lam Marketing & Distribution
**Project ID:** 7143905766777716985

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The atmosphere should be Professional, Dynamic, Execution-Focused, and Premium. We are moving away from traditional "playful creative agency" aesthetics toward a solid "powerhouse" corporate feel. The design must instantly build trust with B2B Marketing and Trade Managers. It should feel robust, modern, and capable of executing nationwide scale brand activations. 

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Deep Slate Navy (#0F172A):** Used as the primary dark theme background or for heavy typography. Represents trust, stability, and large-scale operational capacity.
* **Execution Crimson (#DF2A30):** The primary action color. Represents the dynamic, energetic nature of Vietnamese brand activation and events. Used strictly for Call to Actions (CTAs), primary buttons, and highlighting key metric numbers.
* **Surface White (#FFFFFF):** Core background for light mode and content cards. Ensures maximum readability.
* **Cool Gray (#94A3B8):** Used for secondary reading text, subtitles, and subtle structural borders. 

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Font Family:** "Inter" (A highly legible sans-serif font that excellently supports Vietnamese tonal marks / diacritics as well as English).
* **Headings:** Bold (700-800 weight), often utilizing uppercase for section headers and hero text to emphasize "Scale" and "Authority". Line-height should be tight.
* **Body Text:** Regular (400 weight). The line-height must be slightly taller (e.g., 1.6) to comfortably accommodate the vertical space needed for Vietnamese diacritic marks (dấu câu).

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Sharp, solid, and slightly angular (rounded corner ~2px to 4px max). Avoid overly rounded or pill-shaped buttons; the goal is to look structured, efficient, and corporate.
* **Cards/Containers:** Clean edges with subtle, whisper-soft diffused shadows. Cards should look flat and integrated rather than floating high off the page.
* **Inputs/Forms:** High-contrast minimal borders that light up distinctively upon focus (using Deep Slate Navy or Execution Crimson) to ensure high-conversion on lead-gen forms.

## 5. Layout Principles
* **Whitespace:** Structured, spacious but dense enough to show "action". A clear, robust grid system.
* **Multilingual Consideration:** Layouts must accommodate textual expansion (Vietnamese is often 15-20% longer than equivalent English text). CTAs and Navigation items should have flexible padding to prevent text overflow.

## 6. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation

Create a high-converting B2B agency interface holding the following design constraints:
- Vibe: Premium, Execution-Focused, Corporate Powerhouse. Not an artsy/playful creative agency.

- Primary Action Color: Crimson (#DF2A30)
- Background / Heavy Text Color: Deep Slate Navy (#0F172A)

- Spacing: Clean grid, allowing text to scale gracefully for both Vietnamese and English translations.
